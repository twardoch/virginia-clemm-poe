## Bot Information

**Creator:** @OpenSourceLab

**Description:** Kev evaluates your input as a typed decision and returns a short answer (yes or no) or as specified in the settings. This efficient open-source, low-cost bot is a structured classifier model and the most lightweight in its System One series. You can upload PDF files and ask specific questions about its content. Kev-4B does not support video and audio attachments.

System One models make fast, structured decisions for software applications by returning typed choices instead of free-form text. They are ideal for routing, classification, and other application decision points where speed and predictability matter more than generated prose.

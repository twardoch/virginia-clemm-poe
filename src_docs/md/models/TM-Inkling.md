## Bot Information

**Creator:** @togetherai

**Description:** Inkling is Thinking Machines Lab's first open-weights model, a Mixture-of-Experts transformer with 975B total parameters and 41B active, natively trained to reason over text, images, and audio in a shared hidden space. Each token routes to 6 of 256 experts plus 2 shared experts, with hybrid local and global attention supporting a 256K-token context window. The model varies its thinking effort to balance cost against performance and is built to give calibrated answers rather than confident wrong ones, with 87.9% on GPQA Diamond and 77.6% on SWE-bench Verified among its reported results. Released under Apache 2.0. Available on Together AI.

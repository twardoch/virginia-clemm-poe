**Description:** Tongyi Wanxiang 2.6 (also known as Wan 2.6) is the latest generation of professional-grade AI video and image generation models developed by Alibaba Cloud. It represents a significant leap in AI-driven cinematography and creative storytelling, positioning itself as one of the most comprehensive video generation models globally.

Key Features:

Extended Duration & High Quality: Supports generating videos up to 15 seconds in length with up to 1080P/4K resolution and 24fps, ensuring smooth and cinematic visual quality.

Role-Playing & Identity Consistency: The first model in China to support a "Role-Play" feature. Users can provide a reference image or video of a character/IP, and the model will maintain high visual and vocal consistency across different scenes.

Intelligent Multi-Shot Narrative: Wan 2.6 can understand complex scripts and automatically generate multi-shot videos (storyboarding) while keeping the subjects, environments, and lighting consistent across different camera angles.

Audio-Visual Synchronization: Features native lip-sync and sound generation, allowing characters to speak, sing, or perform with perfectly matched audio and facial expressions.

Cinematic Control: Advanced instruction following for camera movements (e.g., dolly-in, pan, tilt), lighting, and depth of field, enabling professional-level artistic expression.

Versatile Creation Modes: Supports Text-to-Video, Image-to-Video, Video-to-Video, and Voice-to-Video, making it a versatile tool for AI movies, advertising, and social media content.

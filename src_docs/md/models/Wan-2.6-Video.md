## Bot Information

**Creator:** OpenSourceLab

**Description:** Video generation model designed for crafting cinematic, multi-shot narratives, movies and scenes with audio. To add spoken dialogue, wrap the speech in quotation marks (e.g., a painter stand in front his artwork and says: “Art is controlled chaos.”). Wan-2.6-Video is built on Tongyi-Wanxiang-2.6 and can synthesize high-resolution videos at up to 24 FPS with multi shot, character consistency and native audio. The model does support image references.
